# Shipping, Delivery and Returns FAQ

## 1. Standard Delivery Times and Tracking
- **Standard Courier Delivery:** Orders are delivered across metro cities within 2 to 4 business days. Regional and rural areas take 5 to 7 business days.
- **Express Overnight:** Available in select major metropolitan zones for orders placed before 2:00 PM IST.
- **Tracking:** Once dispatched, a live tracking link with airway bill number (AWB) is sent via SMS and email.

## 2. 30-Day Hassle-Free Returns Policy
Hardware items, routers, mesh nodes, and accessories may be returned within 30 days from the confirmed date of delivery under the following conditions:
1. The item is in original physical condition with all bundled cords, power adapters, documentation, and retail packaging.
2. The serial number matches our dispatch records.
3. No customer-induced physical or liquid damage is present.

## 3. Damaged or Defective Deliveries (DOA)
If a product arrives damaged in transit or Dead on Arrival (DOA):
- Report the issue within 48 hours of delivery by opening a support ticket.
- Our team will arrange a free reverse pickup and dispatch an immediate brand-new replacement unit at no extra charge.

## 4. Support Operating Hours
Our digital assistant operates 24/7. Human support specialists and tier-2 escalation engineers are available Monday through Saturday, from 9:00 AM to 8:00 PM IST.
