# Account, Payment and Billing Policy

## 1. Accepted Payment Methods
Support Copilot accepts all major credit and debit cards (Visa, MasterCard, RuPay, American Express), UPI, Net Banking across 45+ banks, and approved corporate purchase orders. All monetary transactions are processed in Indian Rupees (INR).

## 2. Refund Processing and Timelines
- Automatic refund approval is permitted for verified requests up to ₹500.00.
- Refund requests exceeding ₹500.00 are submitted for tier-2 supervisor authorization to prevent fraud and ensure compliance.
- Once issued by our automated system or approved by a representative, funds reflect in the customer's source payment method within 3 to 5 business days for cards and Net Banking, or within 24 hours for UPI.

## 3. Duplicate Charges and Billing Inquiries
If you notice duplicate debit charges on your bank statement for a single order:
1. Provide your Order ID (format: `ORD-XXXXX`).
2. Our billing system verifies transaction logs with the acquiring gateway.
3. Any excess charges will be refunded immediately without deduction of transaction processing fees.

## 4. Subscription Renewal and Invoices
Monthly and annual service plans renew automatically at midnight on the designated billing date. Tax-compliant GST invoices are generated immediately upon settlement and emailed to the registered primary email address.
